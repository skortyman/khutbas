52 Khutba · Draft 1

Aim high, leave the result to Allah

Last updated: 2026-11-13 13:39 EST
First khutba · Opening

إِنَّ الْحَمْدَ لِلَّهِ نَحْمَدُهُ وَنَسْتَعِينُهُ وَنَسْتَغْفِرُهُ وَنَعُوذُ بِاللَّهِ مِنْ شُرُورِ أَنْفُسِنَا وَمِنْ سَيِّئَاتِ أَعْمَالِنَا مَن يَهْدِهِ اللَّهُ فَلَا مُضِلَّ لَهُ وَمَن يُضْلِلْ فَلَا هَادِيَ لَهُ وَأَشْهَدُ أَن لَّا إِلَهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ وَحْدَهُ لَا شَرِيكَ لَهُ وَأَشْهَدُ أَنَّ مُحَمَّدًا عَبْدُهُ وَرَسُولُهُ صَلَّىٰ اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ

All praise is due to Allah. We praise Him, we seek His help, we seek His forgiveness, and we seek refuge in Allah from the evil within ourselves and our evil deeds. Whoever Allah guides, there is none to misguide him. Whoever Allah leads astray, there is none to guide him. I testify there is no god but Allah alone, without any partners, and that Muhammad, peace and blessings be upon him, is His servant and His messenger.

Khutbat al-Hajah · Sunan an-Nasa’i 1404

يَٰأَيُّهَا الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا اتَّقُوا اللَّهَ وَقُولُوا قَوْلًا سَدِيدًا يُصْلِحْ لَكُمْ أَعْمَالَكُمْ وَيَغْفِرْ لَكُمْ ذُنُوبَكُمْ وَمَن يُطِعِ اللَّهَ وَرَسُولَهُ فَقَدْ فَازَ فَوْزًا عَظِيمًا

O you who have believed! Fear Allah and speak words of appropriate justice. He will correct for you your deeds and forgive you your sins. And whoever obeys Allah and His Messenger has certainly attained a great success.

Al-Ahzab 33:70-71

أمَّا بعدُ، فإنَّ أصدقَ الحديثِ كتابُ اللهِ، وخيرَ الهديِ هديُ محمَّدٍ صلَّى اللهُ عليه وسلَّم، وشرَّ الأمورِ مُحدَثاتُها، وكلَّ مُحدَثةٍ بدعةٌ، وكلَّ بدعةٍ ضلالةٌ، وكلَّ ضلالةٍ في النَّارِ

To proceed: As for what follows: the truest speech is the Book of Allah, and the best guidance is the guidance of Muhammad, may Allah bless him and grant him peace. The worst of matters are those newly introduced (into the religion); every newly introduced matter is an innovation, every innovation is misguidance, and every misguidance is in the Fire.

Sunan an-Nasa’i 1578

Two words

  • Earlier this month, a man stood on a stage in Detroit.
    • The first Muslim ever elected to the United States Senate.
    • He told the crowd: all campaign, whenever the outcome came up, he'd said insha'Allah.
    • And that night, the insha'Allah could become alhamdulillah.
Pause · 3s
  • A few weeks before that, my daughter told me something. [Omar: optional, one real detail of where or when]
    • She's scared to set big goals.
    • What if she doesn't reach them?
    • She'd be sad... and she'd feel she let herself down, and let us down.
  • I've been thinking about her question ever since. Because it isn't only hers.
    • The promotion you don't apply for.
    • The business you don't start.
    • The surah you never try to memorize.
  • So one question today: is it safer to aim lower?
  • Let's start with what Allah wants from us.

1Why should we aim high?

  • Ask for the top. The Prophet ﷺ described a hundred levels in Jannah, then said:

فإذا سَأَلْتُمُ اللَّهَ، فاسْأَلُوهُ الفِرْدَوْسَ؛ فإنَّه أوْسَطُ الجَنَّةِ وأَعْلَى الجَنَّةِ

“When you ask Allah, ask Him for al-Firdaws. It's the middle of Jannah and the highest part of Jannah.”

Sahih al-Bukhari 2790
  • He didn't say, ask to just make it in. He said: ask for the top.
  • Allah loves it.

إنَّ اللهَ تعالى يُحِبُّ مَعاليَ الأُمورِ ، و أَشرافَها ، و يَكرَهُ سَفْسافَها

“Allah loves lofty matters and noble ones, and He dislikes the petty ones.”

al-Tabarani · sahih per al-Albani, Sahih al-Jami’ 1890
  • Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz told his friend Raja' ibn Haywah: I have a yearning soul.
    • It yearned to marry Fatimah bint Abd al-Malik, and he married her.
    • It yearned to govern, and he was made governor.
    • It yearned for the caliphate... and he became caliph.
    • “And now my soul yearns for Jannah. And I hope to reach it, insha'Allah.”
  • Every time he reached a goal, he set a higher one.
  • And for the highest goal of all... he said insha'Allah.
  • One guardrail: intention.
    • Actions are judged by intentions. Sahih al-Bukhari 1
    • Aim high for Him, not for the applause.
  • So if Allah loves lofty goals... why is it so hard to set them?

2Why is it so hard?

  • Because it's supposed to be. That's part of why my daughter's big goal feels so heavy.

لَقَدْ خَلَقْنَا ٱلْإِنسَـٰنَ فِى كَبَدٍ

“We have certainly created the human being in toil.”

Al-Balad 90:4

حُفَّتِ الْجَنَّةُ بِالْمَكَارِهِ وَحُفَّتِ النَّارُ بِالشَّهَوَاتِ

“Jannah is surrounded by hardships, and the Fire is surrounded by desires.”

Sahih Muslim 2822
  • Imam al-Nawawi: it's like a curtain. You only reach what's behind it by pushing through it.
  • Ibn al-Qayyim: the wise of every nation agree, comfort is never reached through comfort.
    • Whoever chooses rest... loses rest. Miftah Dar al-Sa’adah
  • So when the big goal feels hard... often, that's the sign you're on the right road.
  • Hard, we can handle. What really stops most of us is something else.

3Why do we hold back?

  • Fear. My daughter's fear: what if I fail?
  • The Prophet ﷺ knew this feeling. He used to say:

اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنَ الْهَمِّ وَالْحَزَنِ، وَالْعَجْزِ وَالْكَسَلِ، وَالْجُبْنِ وَالْبُخْلِ، وَضَلَعِ الدَّيْنِ، وَغَلَبَةِ الرِّجَالِ

“O Allah, I seek refuge in You from worry and grief, from incapacity and laziness, from cowardice and miserliness, from the burden of debt and from being overpowered by people.”

Sahih al-Bukhari 6369
  • Three of those words:
    • الْعَجْزِ al-'ajz: “I can't.”
    • الْكَسَلِ al-kasal: “I could... just not today.”
    • الْجُبْنِ al-jubn: “I could... but what if I fail?”
  • That third one is her question. It's a lot of us. [Omar: optional, a goal you once held back from]
  • And the bravest of people ﷺ still asked Allah for protection from it.
  • So feeling the fear isn't the failure.
  • Letting it choose your goals is.
  • And what happens if we do let it choose?

4What do we lose by aiming low?

  • Small goals don't stay small. They take over. The Prophet ﷺ said:

تَعِسَ عبدُ الدِّينَارِ، وَعَبْدُ الدِّرْهَمِ، وَعَبْدُ الخَمِيصَةِ، إنْ أُعْطِيَ رَضِيَ، وإنْ لَمْ يُعْطَ سَخِطَ

“Wretched is the slave of the dinar, the dirham, and the fine cloak. If he's given, he's pleased. If he isn't, he's angry.”

Sahih al-Bukhari 2887
  • Notice: his whole mood rides on the result.
  • Imam al-Shafi'i: your soul... if you don't keep it busy with truth, it'll keep you busy with falsehood. via Ibn al-Qayyim, al-Jawab al-Kafi
  • So aiming low isn't safe. It just picks a smaller master.
Transition
  • So yes... aim high.
  • But I know what she'd ask next. And it's fair.
  • What if I aim high... and I miss?

I say this saying of mine, and I seek forgiveness from Allah for myself and for you, and for the rest of the Muslims. So ask Him for forgiveness. Indeed, He is the Forgiving, the Merciful.

Second khutba · Opening

بِسْمِ اللَّهِ وَالْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ وَالصَّلَاةُ وَالسَّلَامُ عَلَىٰ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ صَلَّىٰ اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ

In the name of Allah, and all praise is due to Allah, and may Allah's blessings and peace be upon the Messenger of Allah, peace and blessings be upon him.

5How do I handle failure, and success?

  • Before we sat, I left you with her question: what if I aim high and miss?
  • Allah answers both outcomes in one passage:

مَآ أَصَابَ مِن مُّصِيبَةٍ فِى ٱلْأَرْضِ وَلَا فِىٓ أَنفُسِكُمْ إِلَّا فِى كِتَـٰبٍ مِّن قَبْلِ أَن نَّبْرَأَهَآ ۚ إِنَّ ذَٰلِكَ عَلَى ٱللَّهِ يَسِيرٌ ۝ لِّكَيْلَا تَأْسَوْا۟ عَلَىٰ مَا فَاتَكُمْ وَلَا تَفْرَحُوا۟ بِمَآ ءَاتَىٰكُمْ ۗ وَٱللَّهُ لَا يُحِبُّ كُلَّ مُخْتَالٍ فَخُورٍ

“No misfortune strikes the earth or yourselves except that it's in a record before We bring it into being. That is easy for Allah. So that you don't despair over what escaped you, and don't gloat over what He gave you. And Allah doesn't love the arrogant and boastful.”

Al-Hadid 57:22-23
  • When you miss: the Prophet ﷺ gave us the words.

احْرِصْ عَلَى مَا يَنْفَعُكَ، وَاسْتَعِنْ بِاللَّهِ، وَلاَ تَعْجِزْ. وَإِنْ أَصَابَكَ شَىْءٌ فَلاَ تَقُلْ: لَوْ أَنِّي فَعَلْتُ كَانَ كَذَا وَكَذَا، وَلَكِنْ قُلْ: قَدَرُ اللَّهِ وَمَا شَاءَ فَعَلَ؛ فَإِنَّ لَوْ تَفْتَحُ عَمَلَ الشَّيْطَانِ

“Strive for what benefits you, seek Allah's help, and don't give up. And if something befalls you, don't say, ‘If only I had done this, it would have turned out that way.’ Say instead: Allah decreed it, and He does what He wills. Because ‘if only’ opens the door to Shaytan.”

Sahih Muslim 2664
  • Did you hear it? “Don't give up” is la ta'jiz... the same 'ajz from the Prophet's dua.
  • The man from election night lost his first race, in 2018, by more than 20 points.
    • That night, the result didn't come.
    • But the effort that night wasn't wasted.
  • When you win: it's a test too. Sulayman, when he saw the throne set before him:

هَـٰذَا مِن فَضْلِ رَبِّى لِيَبْلُوَنِىٓ ءَأَشْكُرُ أَمْ أَكْفُرُ

“This is from the favor of my Lord, to test me: will I be grateful, or ungrateful?”

An-Naml 27:40 · excerpt
  • Alhamdulillah is how you pass that test.
  • Which brings me back to my daughter.

6So is it better to aim high and miss?

  • Yes. A believer leaves home for hijrah... and dies on the road. He never arrives.

وَمَن يَخْرُجْ مِنۢ بَيْتِهِۦ مُهَاجِرًا إِلَى ٱللَّهِ وَرَسُولِهِۦ ثُمَّ يُدْرِكْهُ ٱلْمَوْتُ فَقَدْ وَقَعَ أَجْرُهُۥ عَلَى ٱللَّهِ

“Whoever leaves his home emigrating to Allah and His Messenger, and death overtakes him, his reward is already secured with Allah.”

An-Nisa 4:100 · excerpt
  • He never reached the goal. His reward is full.
  • And the Prophet ﷺ told us exactly how the angels write it down:

فَمَنْ هَمَّ بِحَسَنَةٍ فَلَمْ يَعْمَلْهَا كَتَبَهَا اللهُ لَهُ عِنْدَهُ حَسَنَةً كَامِلَةً، فَإِنْ هُوَ هَمَّ بِهَا فَعَمِلَهَا كَتَبَهَا اللهُ لَهُ عِنْدَهُ عَشْرَ حَسَنَاتٍ إِلَى سَبْعِمِائَةِ ضِعْفٍ، إِلَى أَضْعَافٍ كَثِيرَةٍ

“Whoever intends a good deed and doesn't do it, Allah writes it with Himself as one full good deed. And if he intends it and does it, Allah writes it as ten good deeds, up to seven hundred times, and many times more.”

Sahih al-Bukhari 6491 · Sahih Muslim 131
A good intentionAchievedNot achieved
Written as10 to 700 times, and more1 full good deed
Pause · 3s
  • Think about what that means:
    • Aim high and reach it... ten to seven hundred times, and more.
    • Aim high and miss... a full good deed, already written.
    • Never aim at all... there's nothing to write.

وَأَن لَّيْسَ لِلْإِنسَـٰنِ إِلَّا مَا سَعَىٰ ۝ وَأَنَّ سَعْيَهُۥ سَوْفَ يُرَىٰ

“A person has only what they strive for, and their striving will be seen.”

An-Najm 53:39-40
  • Ali ibn Abi Talib: the worth of every person is what they do well. Ibn Abd al-Barr, Jami’ Bayan al-‘Ilm
    • That's ihsan. Aim high, then do it well.
  • So here's what I'd tell my daughter now.
  • Allah rewards lofty goals.
  • Allah rewards effort, not outcome.
  • So it's a no-brainer to go for the bigger goal.
  • The reward is guaranteed... even if the outcome isn't.

7So what do we do this week?

  1. Name one big goal by Sunday.
    • Write down the first step.
    • Then say insha'Allah, and mean it.
  2. Learn the Prophet's dua against incapacity, laziness and cowardice.
    • Say it before the hardest thing on your calendar.
  3. Parents and managers: when your child or your team brings you a result...
    • Ask “What did you try?” before you ask “What did you get?”
  • On election night, an insha'Allah became an alhamdulillah.
  • Some of ours will end that way... some will end in qaddar Allah.
  • Both are a believer's words.
  • So... is it safer to aim lower? No.
  • Aim for the highest. Do it well. And leave the result to Allah... insha'Allah.
Second khutba · Conclusion

اللَّهُمَّ صَلِّ عَلَىٰ مُحَمَّدٍ كَمَا صَلَّيْتَ عَلَىٰ إِبْرَاهِيمَ وَعَلَىٰ آلِ إِبْرَاهِيمَ إِنَّكَ حَمِيدٌ مَجِيدٌ

اللَّهُمَّ بَارِكْ عَلَىٰ مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَىٰ آلِ مُحَمَّدٍ كَمَا بَارَكْتَ عَلَىٰ إِبْرَاهِيمَ وَعَلَىٰ آلِ إِبْرَاهِيمَ إِنَّكَ حَمِيدٌ مَجِيدٌ

O Allah, send blessings upon Muhammad as You sent blessings upon Ibrahim and upon the family of Ibrahim. Indeed, You are Praiseworthy and Glorious. O Allah, grant blessings to Muhammad and upon the family of Muhammad as You granted blessings to Ibrahim and upon the family of Ibrahim. Indeed, You are Praiseworthy and Glorious.

Sahih al-Bukhari 3370

لَا يُكَلِّفُ اللَّهُ نَفْسًا إِلَّا وُسْعَهَا ۚ لَهَا مَا كَسَبَتْ وَعَلَيْهَا مَا اكْتَسَبَتْ ۗ رَبَّنَا لَا تُؤَاخِذْنَا إِن نَّسِينَا أَوْ أَخْطَأْنَا ۚ رَبَّنَا وَلَا تَحْمِلْ عَلَيْنَا إِصْرًا كَمَا حَمَلْتَهُ عَلَى الَّذِينَ مِن قَبْلِنَا ۚ رَبَّنَا وَلَا تُحَمِّلْنَا مَا لَا طَاقَةَ لَنَا بِهِ ۖ وَاعْفُ عَنَّا وَاغْفِرْ لَنَا وَارْحَمْنَا ۚ أَنتَ مَوْلَىٰنَا فَانصُرْنَا عَلَى الْقَوْمِ الْكَافِرِينَ

Allah does not require of any soul more than what it can afford. For it are its good deeds and upon it are its bad deeds. Our Lord! Do not hold us accountable if we forget or make a mistake. Our Lord! Do not burden us with that which we have no ability to bear. Pardon us, and forgive us, and have mercy upon us. You are our Master, so help us against the disbelieving people.

Al-Baqarah 2:286
Concluding duas
  • Ya Allah, Ya ‘Aliyy, raise our aims, and make us love what You love of lofty matters.
  • Ya Allah, Ya Qawiyy, protect us from incapacity, from laziness, and from cowardice.
  • Ya Allah, Ya Shakur, accept our effort and count our striving, even when we fall short.
  • Ya Allah, Ya Hakim, when we miss, put qaddar Allah on our tongues, and when we succeed, put alhamdulillah there instead of pride.
  • Ya Allah, Ya Rahman, give our children the courage to aim high and the certainty that You see their effort.
  • Ya Allah, Ya Karim, grant us and our families al-Firdaws al-A’la.

عِبَادَ اللَّهِ إِنَّ اللَّهَ يَأْمُرُ بِالْعَدْلِ وَالْإِحْسَانِ وَإِيتَاءِ ذِي الْقُرْبَىٰ وَيَنْهَىٰ عَنِ الْفَحْشَاءِ وَالْمُنكَرِ وَالْبَغْيِ يَعِظُكُمْ لَعَلَّكُمْ تَذَكَّرُونَ

An-Nahl 16:90

اذْكُرُوا اللَّهَ الْعَظِيمَ يَذْكُرْكُمْ وَاشْكُرُوهُ عَلَىٰ نِعَمِهِ يَزِدْكُمْ وَلَذِكْرُ اللَّهِ أَكْبَرُ وَاللَّهُ يَعْلَمُ مَا تَصْنَعُون

أَقِيمُوا الصَّلَاةَ

Servants of Allah! Indeed, Allah commands justice, the doing of good, and liberality to kith and kin, and He forbids all shameful deeds, and injustice and rebellion. He instructs you, so that you may remember. Remember Allah the Almighty, and He will remember you. And be thankful to Him, and He will increase you in bounty. And the remembrance of Allah is the greatest. And Allah knows all that you do. Establish the prayer.

If there is anything that I have said that is good and you gain value from it, it is surely from Allah. If there is anything I have said that may be incorrect, it is due to my own shortcomings, for that I ask forgiveness from Allah the most merciful and from you.

End of khutba

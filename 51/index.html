AI and the date palms

Last updated: 2026-10-02 11:19 EDT
First khutba · Opening

إِنَّ الْحَمْدَ لِلَّهِ نَحْمَدُهُ وَنَسْتَعِينُهُ وَنَسْتَغْفِرُهُ وَنَعُوذُ بِاللَّهِ مِنْ شُرُورِ أَنْفُسِنَا وَمِنْ سَيِّئَاتِ أَعْمَالِنَا مَن يَهْدِهِ اللَّهُ فَلَا مُضِلَّ لَهُ وَمَن يُضْلِلْ فَلَا هَادِيَ لَهُ وَأَشْهَدُ أَن لَّا إِلَهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ وَحْدَهُ لَا شَرِيكَ لَهُ وَأَشْهَدُ أَنَّ مُحَمَّدًا عَبْدُهُ وَرَسُولُهُ صَلَّىٰ اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ

All praise is due to Allah. We praise Him, we seek His help, we seek His forgiveness, and we seek refuge in Allah from the evil within ourselves and our evil deeds. Whoever Allah guides, there is none to misguide him. Whoever Allah leads astray, there is none to guide him. I testify there is no god but Allah alone, without any partners, and that Muhammad, peace and blessings be upon him, is His servant and His messenger.

Khutbat al-Hajah · Sunan an-Nasa’i 1404

يَٰأَيُّهَا الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا اتَّقُوا اللَّهَ وَقُولُوا قَوْلًا سَدِيدًا يُصْلِحْ لَكُمْ أَعْمَالَكُمْ وَيَغْفِرْ لَكُمْ ذُنُوبَكُمْ وَمَن يُطِعِ اللَّهَ وَرَسُولَهُ فَقَدْ فَازَ فَوْزًا عَظِيمًا

O you who have believed! Fear Allah and speak words of appropriate justice. He will correct for you your deeds and forgive you your sins. And whoever obeys Allah and His Messenger has certainly attained a great success.

Al-Ahzab 33:70-71

أمَّا بعدُ، فإنَّ أصدقَ الحديثِ كتابُ اللهِ، وخيرَ الهديِ هديُ محمَّدٍ صلَّى اللهُ عليه وسلَّم، وشرَّ الأمورِ مُحدَثاتُها، وكلَّ مُحدَثةٍ بدعةٌ، وكلَّ بدعةٍ ضلالةٌ، وكلَّ ضلالةٍ في النَّارِ

To proceed: As for what follows: the truest speech is the Book of Allah, and the best guidance is the guidance of Muhammad, may Allah bless him and grant him peace. The worst of matters are those newly introduced (into the religion); every newly introduced matter is an innovation, every innovation is misguidance, and every misguidance is in the Fire.

Sunan an-Nasa’i 1578

The date palms

When the Prophet ﷺ came to Madinah, he passed farmers pollinating their date palms by hand.

He said, perhaps it would be better if you didn’t.

So they stopped.

They stopped out of love.

That season... the harvest fell.

When they told him, he didn’t scold them. He said two things.

First:

أَنْتُمْ أَعْلَمُ بِأَمْرِ دُنْيَاكُمْ

“You know best the affairs of your worldly life.”

Sahih Muslim 2363

And second:

إِنَّمَا أَنَا بَشَرٌ إِذَا أَمَرْتُكُمْ بِشَيْءٍ مِنْ دِينِكُمْ فَخُذُوا بِهِ وَإِذَا أَمَرْتُكُمْ بِشَيْءٍ مِنْ رَأْيٍ فَإِنَّمَا أَنَا بَشَرٌ

“I am a human being. When I command you about a matter of your religion, accept it. When I command you about something based on my personal opinion, remember that I am merely human.”

Sahih Muslim 2362

Two sentences. The first gives us the orchard. The second tells us where the orchard ends.

Pause · 3s

Today a new tool has arrived in our offices, our schools, and on our kids’ phones. We call it artificial intelligence.

I’ll admit something. Some days, working with it feels like magic. It amplifies what I can do.

Which is exactly why a lot of us are quietly asking one question.

1Are we allowed to build with this?

Yes.

Our jurists hold a simple principle:

الأَصْلُ فِي الأَشْيَاءِ الإِبَاحَةُ

In worldly matters, the default is permission.

Legal maxim · al-aslu fil-ashya’ al-ibahah

Unless Allah or His Messenger ﷺ clearly prohibited it, it’s open to us.

Worship is the opposite. It’s fixed.

We can’t invent a sixth daily prayer. We can’t add a rak’ah to Fajr.

But the dunya? That’s the orchard.

AI is dunya. It’s a tool, like the plow, the compass, the phone in your pocket.

The lesson of the date palms is simple: this part is yours to figure out.

And figuring it out is something we’ve done before.

2Is this new territory for us?

No. When Muslims led, we built the new tools.

Transition

A few years after the date palms, the Prophet ﷺ showed us what that first sentence looks like.

Year five of the Hijrah. Ten thousand troops march on Madinah. The defenders number about three thousand.

Salman al-Farsi brings an idea from Persia: dig a trench.

Nobody in Arabia fought that way.

The Prophet ﷺ didn’t call it foreign. He saw a tool that would save lives... and he picked up a pickaxe himself.

Two centuries later in Baghdad, al-Khwarizmi wrote the book that gave the world algebra.

Europe latinized his name as Algoritmi.

Every AI system on earth runs on algorithms. That word is his name.

In Cairo, Ibn al-Haytham, under house arrest, studied how light enters a dark room.

His Book of Optics laid the foundations of the science behind the camera, including the cameras machines now use to see.

All three were believers. For them, studying creation was worship.

And for centuries, the world followed our lead.

So what changed?

3What happens when we hold back?

Remember the date palms? Here’s the same story at the size of a civilization.

Pause · 3s

Gutenberg’s press arrived in Europe in the 1450s. Books got cheap. Ordinary people started reading.

Within about fifty years, Jewish printers in Istanbul had a press running.

The first Muslim-run press for Arabic script in the empire? Ibrahim Muteferrika, licensed in 1727.

Almost three hundred years later. And even then, only for books that weren’t religious.

Historians still argue about why. No decree banning print has ever been found.

But two reasons come up again and again. Reverence, for the text of the Qur’an and the calligrapher’s art. And protection, for the thousands of scribes who lived by the pen.

Reverence... the same reason the farmers stopped pollinating.

In those centuries, Europe printed hundreds of millions of books. Literacy spread. Science sped up.

The harvest didn’t fall for a season. It fell for nearly three centuries.

When we hold back, the world doesn’t wait.

If we sit out AI, others will decide what it values... and our children will live inside their decisions.

Second khutba · Opening

بِسْمِ اللَّهِ وَالْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ وَالصَّلَاةُ وَالسَّلَامُ عَلَىٰ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ صَلَّىٰ اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ

In the name of Allah, and all praise is due to Allah, and may Allah's blessings and peace be upon the Messenger of Allah, peace and blessings be upon him.

4Does that mean we accept it blindly?

Before we sat, I said: don’t sit this out.

So do we just accept it, eyes closed?

No. Remember the second sentence.

“When I command you about a matter of your religion, accept it.”

The orchard is ours to work. The deen isn’t ours to rewrite, and it isn’t ours to outsource.

Pause · 3s

Earlier I said it feels like magic. It’s not. It’s a pattern engine.

It isn’t a savior, and it isn’t the Dajjal either. It has no soul, no conscience, and it doesn’t fear Allah.

The accountability stays with us. So hold three lines.

First, the truth.

We can clone a voice now. Fake a video. And it lands in the family WhatsApp group by lunch.

Allah tells us in Surah Al-Hujurat:

يَا أَيُّهَا الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا إِنْ جَاءَكُمْ فَاسِقٌ بِنَبَإٍ فَتَبَيَّنُوا أَن تُصِيبُوا قَوْمًا بِجَهَالَةٍ فَتُصْبِحُوا عَلَىٰ مَا فَعَلْتُمْ نَادِمِينَ

“O believers, if an evildoer brings you any news, verify it so you do not harm people unknowingly, becoming regretful for what you have done.”

Al-Hujurat 49:6 · The Clear Quran

Verify before you forward. Every time.

Second, the mind.

If our kids hand their thinking to a prompt, the muscle weakens.

Allah honored us with ‘aql, with reflection, with judgment.

Let it help you think. The thinking is still yours to do.

Third, the sacred.

A chatbot isn’t a mufti.

It predicts words. It doesn’t carry wisdom, mercy, or the fear of standing before Allah.

For the big questions, your marriage, your family, your deen, go to living scholars who do.

Guard the sacred, then. But don’t walk out of the orchard.

5So what do we do this week?

If we leave AI to others, the standards get set by people who may never ask what Allah wants from it.

We need believers building these systems, testing them for bias, using them in medicine, protecting people’s privacy.

It starts small. Three things this week.

  1. Explore it with your kids.
    • This weekend, open an AI tool together.
    • Test it on something you know well. Find where it’s wrong.
    • Raise kids who question it, instead of kids who just consume it.
  2. Win back time at work.
    • Pick one routine task and let a tool take it.
    • Spend that time on your family and your community.
  3. Guard your worship and your mind.
    • Read a real book. Have a real conversation. Sit in quiet reflection.
    • Use the tool. Don’t let the tool use you.

The farmers of Madinah learned two things that season.

The orchard is ours to work.

And the orchard ends where the deen begins.

These tools amplify whatever we bring to them.

So this week, go work your orchard... with ihsan.

Second khutba · Conclusion

اللَّهُمَّ صَلِّ عَلَىٰ مُحَمَّدٍ كَمَا صَلَّيْتَ عَلَىٰ إِبْرَاهِيمَ وَعَلَىٰ آلِ إِبْرَاهِيمَ إِنَّكَ حَمِيدٌ مَجِيدٌ

اللَّهُمَّ بَارِكْ عَلَىٰ مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَىٰ آلِ مُحَمَّدٍ كَمَا بَارَكْتَ عَلَىٰ إِبْرَاهِيمَ وَعَلَىٰ آلِ إِبْرَاهِيمَ إِنَّكَ حَمِيدٌ مَجِيدٌ

O Allah, send blessings upon Muhammad as You sent blessings upon Ibrahim and upon the family of Ibrahim. Indeed, You are Praiseworthy and Glorious. O Allah, grant blessings to Muhammad and upon the family of Muhammad as You granted blessings to Ibrahim and upon the family of Ibrahim. Indeed, You are Praiseworthy and Glorious.

Sahih al-Bukhari 3370

لَا يُكَلِّفُ اللَّهُ نَفْسًا إِلَّا وُسْعَهَا ۚ لَهَا مَا كَسَبَتْ وَعَلَيْهَا مَا اكْتَسَبَتْ ۗ رَبَّنَا لَا تُؤَاخِذْنَا إِن نَّسِينَا أَوْ أَخْطَأْنَا ۚ رَبَّنَا وَلَا تَحْمِلْ عَلَيْنَا إِصْرًا كَمَا حَمَلْتَهُ عَلَى الَّذِينَ مِن قَبْلِنَا ۚ رَبَّنَا وَلَا تُحَمِّلْنَا مَا لَا طَاقَةَ لَنَا بِهِ ۖ وَاعْفُ عَنَّا وَاغْفِرْ لَنَا وَارْحَمْنَا ۚ أَنتَ مَوْلَىٰنَا فَانصُرْنَا عَلَى الْقَوْمِ الْكَافِرِينَ

Allah does not require of any soul more than what it can afford. For it are its good deeds and upon it are its bad deeds. Our Lord! Do not hold us accountable if we forget or make a mistake. Our Lord! Do not burden us with that which we have no ability to bear. Pardon us, and forgive us, and have mercy upon us. You are our Master, so help us against the disbelieving people.

Al-Baqarah 2:286
Concluding duas
  • Ya Allah, Ya ‘Alim, give us knowledge that benefits our families and communities, and protect us from knowledge that doesn’t.
  • Ya Allah, Ya Hakim, give us the wisdom to tell truth from falsehood in an age of fakes.
  • Ya Allah, Ya Rashid, guide our hands and minds as we work the orchard of this world, and keep our intentions anchored in Your pleasure.
  • Ya Allah, Ya Muhsin, inspire our youth to build with excellence and lead with conscience.
  • Ya Allah, Ya Hafiz, protect our children’s minds and hearts from laziness and heedlessness.
  • Ya Allah, Ya Hadi, keep us humble before You, and never let what we can build blind us to how much we need You.

عِبَادَ اللَّهِ إِنَّ اللَّهَ يَأْمُرُ بِالْعَدْلِ وَالْإِحْسَانِ وَإِيتَاءِ ذِي الْقُرْبَىٰ وَيَنْهَىٰ عَنِ الْفَحْشَاءِ وَالْمُنكَرِ وَالْبَغْيِ يَعِظُكُمْ لَعَلَّكُمْ تَذَكَّرُونَ

An-Nahl 16:90

اذْكُرُوا اللَّهَ الْعَظِيمَ يَذْكُرْكُمْ وَاشْكُرُوهُ عَلَىٰ نِعَمِهِ يَزِدْكُمْ وَلَذِكْرُ اللَّهِ أَكْبَرُ وَاللَّهُ يَعْلَمُ مَا تَصْنَعُونَ

أَقِيمُوا الصَّلَاةَ

Servants of Allah! Indeed, Allah commands justice, the doing of good, and liberality to kith and kin, and He forbids all shameful deeds, and injustice and rebellion. He instructs you, so that you may remember. Remember Allah the Almighty, and He will remember you. And be thankful to Him, and He will increase you in bounty. And the remembrance of Allah is the greatest. And Allah knows all that you do. Establish the prayer.

If there is anything that I have said that is good and you gain value from it, it is surely from Allah. If there is anything I have said that may be incorrect, it is due to my own shortcomings, for that I ask forgiveness from Allah the most merciful and from you.

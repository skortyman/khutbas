First khutba · Opening

إِنَّ الْحَمْدَ لِلَّهِ نَحْمَدُهُ وَنَسْتَعِينُهُ وَنَسْتَغْفِرُهُ وَنَعُوذُ بِاللَّهِ مِنْ شُرُورِ أَنْفُسِنَا وَمِنْ سَيِّئَاتِ أَعْمَالِنَا مَن يَهْدِهِ اللَّهُ فَلَا مُضِلَّ لَهُ وَمَن يُضْلِلْ فَلَا هَادِيَ لَهُ وَأَشْهَدُ أَن لَّا إِلَهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ وَحْدَهُ لَا شَرِيكَ لَهُ وَأَشْهَدُ أَنَّ مُحَمَّدًا عَبْدُهُ وَرَسُولُهُ صَلَّىٰ اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ

All praise is due to Allah. We praise Him, we seek His help, we seek His forgiveness, and we seek refuge in Allah from the evil within ourselves and our evil deeds. Whoever Allah guides, there is none to misguide him. Whoever Allah leads astray, there is none to guide him. I testify there is no god but Allah alone, without any partners, and that Muhammad, peace and blessings be upon him, is His servant and His messenger.

Khutbat al-Hajah · Sunan an-Nasa’i 1404

يَٰأَيُّهَا الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا اتَّقُوا اللَّهَ وَقُولُوا قَوْلًا سَدِيدًا يُصْلِحْ لَكُمْ أَعْمَالَكُمْ وَيَغْفِرْ لَكُمْ ذُنُوبَكُمْ وَمَن يُطِعِ اللَّهَ وَرَسُولَهُ فَقَدْ فَازَ فَوْزًا عَظِيمًا

O you who have believed! Fear Allah and speak words of appropriate justice. He will correct for you your deeds and forgive you your sins. And whoever obeys Allah and His Messenger has certainly attained a great success.

Al-Ahzab 33:70-71

أمَّا بعدُ، فإنَّ أصدقَ الحديثِ كتابُ اللهِ، وخيرَ الهديِ هديُ محمَّدٍ صلَّى اللهُ عليه وسلَّم، وشرَّ الأمورِ مُحدَثاتُها، وكلَّ مُحدَثةٍ بدعةٌ، وكلَّ بدعةٍ ضلالةٌ، وكلَّ ضلالةٍ في النَّارِ

To proceed: As for what follows: the truest speech is the Book of Allah, and the best guidance is the guidance of Muhammad, may Allah bless him and grant him peace. The worst of matters are those newly introduced (into the religion); every newly introduced matter is an innovation, every innovation is misguidance, and every misguidance is in the Fire.

Sunan an-Nasa’i 1578

2Tafseer Surah Al-Ma’un

Source: Tafseer Al-Shawkani, Nouman Ali Khan

أَرَأَيْتَ الَّذِي يُكَذِّبُ بِالدِّينِ

Have you seen him who denies the Recompense?

Al-Ma’un 107:1
  • الدِّين deen = Islam
  • الدِّين deen = recompense (payback); day of resurrection
    • people afraid of consequences; easier to do bad things when there are no consequences to be afraid of

فَذَٰلِكَ الَّذِي يَدُعُّ الْيَتِيمَ

For that is the one who drives away the orphan

Al-Ma’un 107:2
  • يَدُعُّ yadu’a = push someone with expectation that they will not push back; bully!
  • فَذَٰلِكَ fa-zalik: used to belittle, put distance between oneself and the object, “that one”, “over there”
  • Quraish didn’t give inheritance to women and children
  • الْمِسْكِين، الْيَتِيم miskin, yatim: Allah uses singular, so oppressors think of specific people

وَلَا يَحُضُّ عَلَىٰ طَعَامِ الْمِسْكِينِ

And does not encourage the feeding of the poor.

Al-Ma’un 107:3
  • طَعَامِ الْمِسْكِينِ ta’aam al-miskin: food belongs to the poor, not for the poor

فَوَيْلٌ لِّلْمُصَلِّينَ

So woe to those who pray

Al-Ma’un 107:4
  • وَيْلٌ wayl: also used to admonish the one who cheats in business (al-mutaffifoon) and the one who writes something and falsely attributes it to Allah (humaza)
  • الْمُصَلِّينَ al-musalloon: Allah ends the Ayah here and wants us to think: “Is he talking about me?”
  • People that commit crimes against each other are likely to commit crimes against Allah

In next section of surah, Allah changes to plural

الَّذِينَ هُمْ عَن صَلَاتِهِمْ سَاهُونَ

Who are they that are unmindful of their prayer

Al-Ma’un 107:5

سَاهُونَ sahoon:

  • sajda sahwa; to forget, act out of neglectfulness, carelessness; not paying enough attention, treating as a priority;
  • will pray when it’s convenient
  • not doing wudu properly, not praying on time; praying quickly;
  • praying feels like an obstacle
  • عَن صَلَاتِهِمْ ‘an salatihim: about salah, not during salah; getting distracted during salah is a different matter

الَّذِينَ هُمْ يُرَاءُونَ

Those who make show [of their deeds]

Al-Ma’un 107:6

Show off:

  • الرِّيَاء riya: arrogance is harder to spot than a black ant on a black stone on a dark night
  • scholars say to strike balance; pray fard in mosque and extras at home

وَيَمْنَعُونَ الْمَاعُونَ

And withhold [simple] assistance.

Al-Ma’un 107:7

الْمَاعُون ma’un:

  • something minor: the loss of which would not decrease your wealth
  • borrow bucket, water, utensils
  • you can’t possibly be cheap on it
  • only reason not to share: just don’t want others to benefit

Second khutba · Conclusion

اللَّهُمَّ صَلِّ عَلَىٰ مُحَمَّدٍ كَمَا صَلَّيْتَ عَلَىٰ إِبْرَاهِيمَ وَعَلَىٰ آلِ إِبْرَاهِيمَ إِنَّكَ حَمِيدٌ مَجِيدٌ

اللَّهُمَّ بَارِكْ عَلَىٰ مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَىٰ آلِ مُحَمَّدٍ كَمَا بَارَكْتَ عَلَىٰ إِبْرَاهِيمَ وَعَلَىٰ آلِ إِبْرَاهِيمَ إِنَّكَ حَمِيدٌ مَجِيدٌ

O Allah, send blessings upon Muhammad as You sent blessings upon Ibrahim and upon the family of Ibrahim. Indeed, You are Praiseworthy and Glorious. O Allah, grant blessings to Muhammad and upon the family of Muhammad as You granted blessings to Ibrahim and upon the family of Ibrahim. Indeed, You are Praiseworthy and Glorious.

Sahih al-Bukhari 3370

لَا يُكَلِّفُ اللَّهُ نَفْسًا إِلَّا وُسْعَهَا ۚ لَهَا مَا كَسَبَتْ وَعَلَيْهَا مَا اكْتَسَبَتْ ۗ رَبَّنَا لَا تُؤَاخِذْنَا إِن نَّسِينَا أَوْ أَخْطَأْنَا ۚ رَبَّنَا وَلَا تَحْمِلْ عَلَيْنَا إِصْرًا كَمَا حَمَلْتَهُ عَلَى الَّذِينَ مِن قَبْلِنَا ۚ رَبَّنَا وَلَا تُحَمِّلْنَا مَا لَا طَاقَةَ لَنَا بِهِ ۖ وَاعْفُ عَنَّا وَاغْفِرْ لَنَا وَارْحَمْنَا ۚ أَنتَ مَوْلَىٰنَا فَانصُرْنَا عَلَى الْقَوْمِ الْكَافِرِينَ

Allah does not require of any soul more than what it can afford. For it are its good deeds and upon it are its bad deeds. Our Lord! Do not hold us accountable if we forget or make a mistake. Our Lord! Do not burden us with that which we have no ability to bear. Pardon us, and forgive us, and have mercy upon us. You are our Master, so help us against the disbelieving people.

Al-Baqarah 2:286
Concluding duas
  • Ya Allah, Ya Malik, Master of the Day of Recompense, keep that Day before our eyes, so we never take lightly Your rights or the rights of Your creation.
  • Ya Allah, Ya Rahman, let our five pillars hold up a whole building: worship of You, and service to Your creation. Give us balance between the two.
  • Ya Allah, Ya Ra’uf, soften our hearts toward the orphan and the poor. Never let us push them away, and make us people who feed them and urge others to feed them.
  • Ya Allah, Ya Hafiz, help us guard our prayers: on time, with proper wudu, with our hearts present, and not only when it’s convenient.
  • Ya Allah, Ya ‘Alim, You know what’s hidden in our hearts. Purify our deeds of showing off, and make them sincerely for You.
  • Ya Allah, Ya Karim, make us generous with the small things, a bucket, a cup of water, a helping hand, and never let us withhold them from anyone in need.
  • Ya Allah, Ya Wakil, we put our trust in You. Help us tie our camels too, and show up for our neighbors of every faith when they’re in need.

عِبَادَ اللَّهِ إِنَّ اللَّهَ يَأْمُرُ بِالْعَدْلِ وَالْإِحْسَانِ وَإِيتَاءِ ذِي الْقُرْبَىٰ وَيَنْهَىٰ عَنِ الْفَحْشَاءِ وَالْمُنكَرِ وَالْبَغْيِ يَعِظُكُمْ لَعَلَّكُمْ تَذَكَّرُونَ

An-Nahl 16:90

اذْكُرُوا اللَّهَ الْعَظِيمَ يَذْكُرْكُمْ وَاشْكُرُوهُ عَلَىٰ نِعَمِهِ يَزِدْكُمْ وَلَذِكْرُ اللَّهِ أَكْبَرُ وَاللَّهُ يَعْلَمُ مَا تَصْنَعُونَ

أَقِيمُوا الصَّلَاةَ

Servants of Allah! Indeed, Allah commands justice, the doing of good, and liberality to kith and kin, and He forbids all shameful deeds, and injustice and rebellion. He instructs you, so that you may remember. Remember Allah the Almighty, and He will remember you. And be thankful to Him, and He will increase you in bounty. And the remembrance of Allah is the greatest. And Allah knows all that you do. Establish the prayer.

If there is anything that I have said that is good and you gain value from it, it is surely from Allah. If there is anything I have said that may be incorrect, it is due to my own shortcomings, for that I ask forgiveness from Allah the most merciful and from you.
